About Me

Hi, I'm Margie.
Your local driving instructor.

With over 15 years of experience teaching learner drivers, I've helped hundreds of students gain the confidence and skills to pass their test first time. I'm a fully qualified, DVSA-approved driving instructor passionate about road safety and helping you become a safe, independent driver.

My lessons are tailored to your individual needs - whether you're anxious behind the wheel or a quick learner ready for your test. I use a dual-controlled car for your safety, and I'll always move at a pace you're comfortable with.

  • DVSA Approved Driving Instructor (ADI)
  • Patient, calm and friendly approach
  • Manual lessons - Dorchester, Bridport & Beaminster
  • Dual-controlled vehicle for your safety

What We Do

Everything you need to get on the road

From your very first lesson to passing your test and beyond - Allen's Driving School has a course to suit every learner.

Beginner Lessons

Perfect if you've never sat in the driver's seat before. We start with the basics - controls, safety, and building confidence at low speeds in quiet areas.

Intensive Courses

Need to pass quickly? Our intensive crash courses pack weeks of learning into just days, ideal if you're on a deadline or simply learn faster in longer blocks.

Pass Plus

Newly passed? The Pass Plus course covers motorways, rural roads, and night driving - making you a safer, more well-rounded driver and potentially lowering your insurance.

Theory Test Preparation

Guidance on hazard perception, highway code, and mock questions so you walk into the theory test fully prepared and confident.

Motorway Driving

Many new drivers feel uneasy on motorways. We'll cover lane discipline, joining and leaving, smart motorways, and safe following distances.

Refresher Lessons

Been off the road for a while? Refresher lessons help you rebuild confidence, iron out bad habits, and get you comfortable driving again at any experience level.
